# Tamil Nadu Development Lens — 2031

## Purpose
Translate published civic or political intentions **without inventing endorsement** into measurable public-development outcomes that residents can assess independently. Research should remain useful regardless of electoral outcomes.

## One development compact
1. Exact public intention and attribution, or explicitly labelled **independent proposed objective**.
2. Development outcome that people experience, not an event count or social metric.
3. Dated indicator definition, geography, official baseline, data caveats and a proposed 2031 target **only after review**.
4. Alternative policy levers and the public body or partner with actual power to implement.
5. Fiscal envelope, ongoing costs, administrative constraints and trade-offs.
6. Small cooperation-based pilot with baseline, comparison, measurement and corrective review.
7. Outcome publication, contrary findings and source-linked corrections.

## Initial source context, as of 22 October 2026

- SDG Index 2023–24: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2032859; score 78/100 is historical, not a current 2026 measurement.
- Tamil Nadu DES statistical handbooks: https://www.des.tn.gov.in/en/node/32; district data requires systematic ingestion and unit/period checks.
- Tamil Nadu SDG framework: https://des.tn.gov.in/en/node/404; the state already has an SDG-localisation programme.
- We The Leaders public programme descriptions: https://www.wetheleader.org/policy-papers; published themes are **not automatically 2031 pledges**.

## Interpretation and limitations
All seven outcome compacts are **research candidates**. Their quantitative starting values and 2031 targets are deliberately null. Imported social-post metrics identify published discussion, not representative public opinion. Do not capture private health, student, complainant, or personal-location records. A civic movement may enable civic action, but cannot promise work belonging to a separate statutory body. Budget totals do not represent discretionary funding for new programmes.

## Acceptance criteria for the next release
- Two official district indicators ingested with year, unit, denominator, source page, missingness and caveats.
- One policy compact has an externally reviewed baseline and an auditable funding/institutional feasibility assessment.
- One consenting local-body public-service pilot has a baseline, complaint-routing agreement, defined measure and independent follow-up.
- Every revised conclusion retains source, reviewer and correction history.
- No unverified number is rendered as a confirmed impact claim.

Out of scope: electoral persuasion, voter-level analytics, candidate/party rankings and future-election predictions.
